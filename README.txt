TIKTOK FRIEND BOT
=================
1. Double-click start.bat (first time it installs for about a minute).
2. An Edge window opens on TikTok. Log in to the BOT's TikTok account (a spare one, not your main).
3. Notepad opens your settings (.env). Fill in OWNER_NAME, OWNER_TIKTOK (your username) and GROQ_API_KEY
   (free at https://console.groq.com/keys). Save, then close the black window and run start.bat again.
4. Make sure your account and the bot account follow each other, then DM the bot from your account.

Keep the black window and the Edge window open while you want the bot online.
